Why Use Compress PDF?

PDF documents can quickly become heavy in size, especially when they include high-resolution images, scanned pages, or complex graphics. A Compress PDF tool helps reduce the overall file size while keeping the document clear and readable. This makes it much easier to handle large files without losing important content.

Using a PDF compression tool is helpful when you need to upload documents to websites, send files through email, or store files on limited storage space. Smaller PDFs load faster, transfer quicker, and are more convenient to manage across devices.

Instead of editing the file manually, a compress PDF tool automatically optimizes the document. With just a quick upload, your file becomes lighter and easier to share while maintaining the original formatting and page structure.

Features

Everything in One PDF Compressor

📉
Image Resampling
Embedded raster images are downsampled to your target DPI and re-encoded at the chosen quality — the single largest source of size reduction in typical PDFs.
🔤
Font Subsetting
Full embedded fonts are trimmed to include only the characters actually used in your document — often saving hundreds of kilobytes per font.
🧹
Metadata Stripping
Author name, edit history, embedded thumbnails, and hidden annotation data are scrubbed — protecting your privacy and trimming unnecessary bulk.
♻️
Object Deduplication
Duplicate object streams — common in PDFs built from templates or merged documents — are identified and collapsed to a single reference.
🌑
Grayscale Conversion
Optionally convert all embedded colour images to grayscale — ideal for documents where colour is decorative and file size is the priority.
📦
Batch Processing

100% Private
