FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an AI image enhancer?
An AI image enhancer is a tool that uses artificial intelligence to automatically improve photo quality by sharpening details, increasing resolution, and reducing blur or noise.
How does an AI image enhancer improve photo quality?
It analyzes the image using machine learning algorithms and enhances details, adjusts brightness, improves sharpness, and restores missing pixels in low-resolution photos.
Can AI enhance blurry images?
Yes, many AI image enhancers can reduce blur and improve clarity by reconstructing image details and increasing resolution.
Is it safe to enhance photos online?
Most modern image enhancer tools process images securely in the browser and do not permanently store uploaded photos.
What types of images can be enhanced using AI?
You can enhance portraits, product images, social media photos, old pictures, and even low-resolution screenshots.
Why should I use an AI image enhancer instead of manual editing?
AI tools work automatically and save time by improving image quality instantly without requiring advanced photo editing skills.
