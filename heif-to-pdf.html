Why Use HEIF to PDF Converter?

HEIF (High Efficiency Image Format) is used by many modern smartphones for storing high-quality photos. While this format offers excellent compression, it is not supported by all applications and devices.

A HEIF to PDF converter helps transform these images into a document format that works everywhere.

By converting HEIF images into PDF, users can easily share photos or image-based documents without compatibility issues. This ensures that files remain viewable across multiple platforms and devices.

Features

Everything in One HEIF to PDF Converter

📷
HEIC & HEIF Support
Both .heic (iPhone/iPad photos) and .heif (general HEIF container) files are accepted. The browser's native HEIF decoder handles the HEVC-compressed image data without any additional library.
🔄
EXIF Orientation Correction
EXIF rotation tags (1–8) are read from the file header and applied as canvas transformations before embedding — every photo appears upright in the PDF, even when shot in portrait mode.
✨
HDR Tone Mapping
iPhone 12+ photos shot in HDR contain extended-range luminance data. The HDR tone mapping option compresses the extended range into the standard SDR range for correct rendering in PDF viewers.
↕️
Drag-to-Reorder
Drag photo thumbnails to rearrange page order in the PDF — set a custom sequence for photo albums, portfolios, and multi-photo documents without renaming or re-uploading files.
📐
Three Layout Modes
One photo per page at native dimensions, Fit to page (scaled to any paper size), or Contact sheet (multiple photos per page in a grid — ideal for proof sheets and photo logs).
📅
Photo Date Captions
Optionally include the photo's capture date as a caption beneath each image in the PDF — useful for photo albums, travel journals, and documentation workflows.
📦
Batch Conversion
Upload an entire album of HEIC photos at once — all are decoded, thumbnailed, and assembled into a single PDF in the order you set via the drag-and-drop grid.
🔒
100% Private
HEIF decoding and PDF assembly happen entirely in browser memory — your photos never leave your device. No upload, no cloud, no server. Your personal photos stay personal.
