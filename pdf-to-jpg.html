Why Use PDF to JPG Converter?

A PDF to JPG converter helps transform document pages into one of the most widely used image formats on the internet. JPG files are lightweight, easy to upload, and compatible with almost all devices and platforms.

This conversion is useful when you need to extract images, charts, or document snapshots from a PDF file. Instead of sharing an entire document, you can send a single image that clearly displays the information.

For bloggers, designers, and digital marketers, converting PDF pages into JPG images makes it easier to use document visuals in websites, presentations, and social media content.

Pro Tips

Get the Best Results

Choosing the Right DPI
tip → resolution
Use 72 DPI for web previews and email-friendly thumbnails. 150 DPI is ideal for screen display and most digital uses. Use 300 DPI only when you need print-quality sharpness — it produces significantly larger files.
JPG vs PNG Output
tip → format
JPG is best for photos and document pages with gradients — smaller files, barely any visible difference. Choose PNG when you need perfect sharpness for text, logos, or diagrams, or when you need a transparent background.
Selecting Specific Pages
tip → pages
Click on any thumbnail to toggle its selection. Use "Select All" and "Deselect All" shortcuts for bulk actions. The badge next to "Select Pages" always shows how many pages are currently selected.
Password-Protected PDFs
tip → security
This tool works with unlocked PDFs. If your PDF is password-protected, you'll need to unlock it first using a PDF editor or the print-to-PDF trick (open in a viewer, print, save as PDF) before uploading.
Large PDFs
tip → performance
For PDFs with 50+ pages at 300 DPI, rendering can take 10–20 seconds depending on your device. Preview thumbnails are generated at low resolution first — conversion runs at your selected DPI when you click Extract.
Transparent Background
tip → background
Enabling transparent background only applies when exporting as PNG. Transparent PDFs (those with no background fill) will render with a transparent canvas — useful for overlaying on other designs.
