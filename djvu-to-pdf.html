FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a DJVU file?
DJVU is a document format commonly used for scanned books and documents because it compresses images efficiently.
Why convert DJVU files to PDF?
PDF is a more widely supported format that can be opened easily on most devices and software.
Will converting DJVU to PDF change document quality?
Most converters preserve page structure and image clarity during the conversion process.
Can scanned documents in DJVU format be converted to PDF?
Yes, scanned pages stored in DJVU format can be converted into standard PDF documents.
Do I need special software to open DJVU files?
Yes, DJVU files often require specific readers, which is why converting them to PDF is convenient.
Is DJVU to PDF conversion safe online?
Trusted online converters process files securely and often delete them automatically after conversion.
