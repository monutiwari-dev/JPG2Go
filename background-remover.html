Features

Everything in One Background Remover

🖱️
Click-to-Sample
Click directly on the preview image to sample background colours. Add as many sample points as needed for complex or uneven backgrounds.
🎚️
Tolerance Control
Adjust how similar a colour must be to the sampled background to be removed — covers gradients and slight variations without over-erasing the subject.
🌊
Flood Fill
Connected-region algorithm removes only background pixels touching the image border — preserves interior areas of the same colour that belong to the subject.
✨
Edge Feathering
Gaussian alpha falloff at cut boundaries produces smooth, anti-aliased edges — no jagged pixel edges, no white fringing around fine detail.
🎨
Background Replace
Replace the removed background with transparent, white, black, grey, or any custom hex colour — directly in the same operation.
📦
Batch Processing
Apply the same removal settings to a whole folder of images at once — consistent results across product catalogues, headshots, or icon sets.
🔒
100% Private
Your images never leave your device. No server, no cloud, no account. The Canvas API processes everything locally in your browser tab.
📄
PNG Output
Transparent outputs are automatically saved as PNG to preserve the alpha channel. Solid-background outputs are saved in the original format.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an online background remover?
A background remover is a tool that automatically removes the background from an image and isolates the main subject.
How does an AI background remover work?
AI algorithms detect the subject of the image and separate it from the background using object recognition technology.
Can I create transparent images with a background remover?
Yes, most background remover tools allow you to export the image with a transparent PNG background.
Is background removal useful for ecommerce?
Yes, ecommerce stores use background removal to create clean product photos for marketplaces and online catalogs.
Can I replace the background after removing it?
Many tools allow you to add a new background, color, or graphic after removing the original background.
What image formats work best with background remover tools?
Common formats like JPG, PNG, and WebP usually work best for automatic background removal.
