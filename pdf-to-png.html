Why Use PDF to PNG Converter?

A PDF to PNG converter enables users to convert document pages into high-quality image files with excellent clarity. PNG format is known for preserving sharp text and graphics, making it ideal for screenshots and digital publishing.

This type of conversion is especially useful for tutorials, blog posts, and visual guides where document content needs to be displayed as images.

Because PNG images maintain high resolution, they ensure that text and diagrams remain clear even when viewed on different screen sizes.

Pro Tips

Get the Best Results

PNG vs JPG for PDF extraction
tip → format choice
Choose PNG when your PDF contains sharp text, line art, diagrams, or logos — lossless compression means no artefacts. Use JPG when file size matters more than pixel-perfection, such as photo-heavy pages for web use.
When Transparent Really Works
tip → transparency
Transparent background works best on PDF pages that were created without a background fill object — diagrams, vector illustrations, icon exports. Pages with a drawn white rectangle as background will still appear white even in transparent mode, since that rectangle is part of the page content.
Choosing the Right DPI
tip → resolution
72 DPI is fine for web thumbnails and Slack previews. 150 DPI delivers sharp results for screen presentations and most digital uses. Use 300 DPI for print-quality output or when you'll be zooming in closely — note that file sizes grow significantly at 300 DPI.
Selecting Specific Pages
tip → page picker
Click any thumbnail to toggle selection. The badge next to "Select Pages" shows how many pages are queued. Use "Deselect All" followed by individual clicks to pick non-contiguous pages quickly, such as every other page or just chapter openers.
Large PDFs at 300 DPI
tip → performance
At 300 DPI, each page canvas is roughly 3.5× wider and taller than at 150 DPI — meaning around 12× more pixels to process. For PDFs with 20+ pages at 300 DPI, rendering can take 20–40 seconds on mobile. Use a desktop browser for the best performance on large batches.
Password-Protected PDFs
tip → security
This tool opens unlocked PDFs only. If your PDF requires a password to open, you'll need to remove the protection first using a PDF editor or the print-to-PDF method — open in a viewer that accepts the password, then print/save as a new PDF before uploading here.
