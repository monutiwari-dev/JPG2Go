Why Use EPUB to PDF Converter?

EPUB files are designed for digital reading devices and allow flexible text layouts. However, EPUB files are not always suitable when you need a fixed document structure.

A EPUB to PDF converter helps turn eBook content into a stable document format that can be printed or shared easily.

This conversion ensures that the layout remains consistent regardless of device or screen size. Converting EPUB to PDF is useful when you want to archive or distribute digital books in a more standardized format.

Important Note

DRM-Protected EPUBs Cannot Be Converted

🔓
DRM-Free EPUBs
EPUBs from Project Gutenberg, Standard Ebooks, open-access publishers, and your own DRM-free purchases can be freely converted with Calibre and the methods above.
🔒
Adobe DRM EPUBs
Most ebooks purchased from major retailers (Amazon, Kobo, Google Play, Apple Books) are protected by Adobe Digital Editions DRM. These cannot be converted by Calibre or any other tool without removing the DRM, which may violate copyright law in your country.
📋
Check for DRM
Try opening the EPUB in Calibre — if it asks for a password or shows an error about protection, the file is DRM-protected. DRM-free EPUBs open and convert without any password prompt.
⚖️
Legal Note
Converting a DRM-free EPUB you own for personal use on your own devices is generally legal in most countries. Converting and distributing copyrighted content is not. Always respect the copyright and licensing terms of the eBook you are converting.
Pro Tips

Getting the Best EPUB to PDF Output

Calibre Output Settings
tip → calibre
In Calibre's conversion dialog, go to the PDF Output section and set: Paper size (A4 for European, Letter for US), custom margins (25mm all sides works well), and font size base (11 or 12 pt for body text). The "Look and feel" section lets you control line spacing and serif vs sans-serif font defaults.
Table of Contents
tip → TOC
Enable "Add Table of Contents to PDF" in Calibre's PDF Output settings. For EPUB3 files with proper navigation, this adds a clickable TOC to the output PDF — essential for longer books. For EPUB2 files the result depends on how the original TOC was structured.
Custom Fonts
tip → fonts
EPUBs with custom embedded fonts may not render correctly in Calibre if those fonts aren't installed on your system. In the "Look and feel" section of Calibre's conversion dialog, you can override the font family to use one you have installed — Palatino and Georgia work well for book-style PDFs.
Batch Conversion
tip → batch
Calibre supports batch conversion — select multiple EPUB files, then Convert books → Bulk Convert. Apply the same PDF output settings to the whole batch. On Linux, the ebook-convert CLI lets you loop over a directory: for f in *.epub; do ebook-convert "$f" "${f%.epub}.pdf"; done
FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an EPUB to PDF converter?
An EPUB to PDF converter is a tool that converts digital ebook files into PDF format for easier reading and sharing.
Why convert EPUB ebooks into PDF format?
PDF files are widely supported across devices, while EPUB files require specific ebook reader applications.
Will the ebook formatting remain after EPUB to PDF conversion?
Most converters preserve the original text structure, chapters, and formatting when converting EPUB files into PDF documents.
Can EPUB files contain images and formatting?
Yes, EPUB files often include images, structured text, and navigation elements that are preserved during conversion.
Is EPUB to PDF conversion useful for printing ebooks?
Yes, converting EPUB files into PDF makes them easier to print because PDF format supports fixed page layouts.
Can I read the converted PDF on any device?
Yes, once converted into PDF, the ebook can be opened on smartphones, tablets, computers, and most PDF readers.
